# Formie Editorial Product Redesign

## Direction

Rebuild the marketing homepage as one continuous cream, black, and gold product story. The visual language should feel editorial and precise rather than template-like: oversized app screens, overlapping layers, restrained borders, asymmetric composition, and a confident grotesk/condensed type pairing.

## Homepage

- Remove the sample-analysis link, Progress section, Full-set Analysis section, and generic numbered “Record / Understand / Improve” cards.
- Make the hero phone composition substantially larger, shift it left into the copy boundary, and remove the visible white image container/cutoff.
- Replace isolated image cards with full-bleed compositions whose cream or black backdrops match their containing section.
- Turn How It Works into a visual recording-to-review journey with text integrated beside—not over—the important parts of—the screens.
- Present coaching as a six-domain review: surroundings, equipment/setup, grip/contact, starting position, execution, and support/balance.
- Include a dedicated setup-and-execution guide preview so the website matches the new in-app result.
- Redesign the What happened / Why it matters / What to do next modules as compact editorial annotations with one highlighted active state, not three identical generated-looking boxes.
- Build a richer Formie Pro composition with layered plan details, usage allowance, Coach access, early premium access, priority support, and surrounding app screens. Keep the offer exactly “$10 for 10 analyses.”
- Use more real product states throughout: dashboard, recording, issue review, setup guide, six-domain audit, Formie Coach, and saved analyses.
- Keep the final support/download call-to-action and legal footer. No fake social links.

## Typography and Responsiveness

Use Geist for body/UI copy and Archivo Black for display headlines. Keep display type compact enough to avoid awkward mobile wrapping. At 320–430 px, screens stack intentionally, navigation becomes compact, and all artwork stays within the viewport without horizontal scrolling.

## Images

Generated product compositions must be based on the supplied Formie app references, use the exact section background color, and avoid framed white rectangles, fake device shadows, watermarks, or unrelated UI. Critical pricing and coaching text remains HTML for accuracy and accessibility.

## Acceptance

The one-page contract, lint, and production build pass. Browser checks cover 320 px, 390 px, and desktop layouts with no horizontal overflow. The deployed apex site contains the revised sections and canonical metadata, and `www` redirects to the apex.
