# Formie 1.0 compliance package

## Release position

- United States storefront only; no EU storefronts for 1.0.
- Users must be at least 18. Formie is educational fitness feedback, not diagnosis, treatment, or physical therapy.
- Formie Monthly is the only offered plan: $9.99 USD per month, 10 analyses per monthly quota period, no carryover.
- Manual App Store release after approval.
- Formie Coach is a static preview, has no controls, and is not included in the subscription.
- Canonical public support identity: `support@useformie.com`.

## Processors and data flows

- Supabase: authentication, database, private storage, Edge Functions, consent ledger, and encrypted cleanup queue.
- Paid Google Gemini API: exercise video, exercise declaration, relevant profile context, and analysis instructions only after affirmative consent. Production generation and uploads fail closed unless the billing-enabled project and disabled voluntary log sharing are explicitly confirmed. The current analysis models are pinned to the official stable IDs `gemini-3.7-flash` and `gemini-3.1-flash-lite`; preview, experimental, version-suffixed, and mutable `latest` aliases are rejected. Cleanup-only paths can still delete provider files while generation is disabled.
- YouTube Data API: canonical catalog exercise name only; no user-linked data. Public metadata is globally cached and revalidated within 24 hours. An hourly database expiry job enforces the maximum 30-day lifetime independently of user traffic.
- RevenueCat and Apple: purchase, entitlement, subscription-management, and Sign in with Apple state.
- Resend: only feedback or support messages the user chooses to submit through the existing support delivery path.
- Vercel: public Formie website and legal/support pages.

## Privacy and deletion

The machine-readable App Privacy declaration is [privacy-labels.json](./privacy-labels.json). Tracking is No. New-account creation presents a separate affirmative AI-processing choice apart from Terms acceptance; existing accounts without current consent are asked before entering recording, and reanalysis after withdrawal asks again. The notice identifies the data, paid Gemini recipient, purpose, limited safety retention, and deletion controls. Dismissal sends nothing.

Account deletion snapshots provider identifiers first, revokes or queues available Sign in with Apple credentials, deletes or queues Gemini files and the RevenueCat customer, then removes Formie storage, analytics, database rows, and Auth. Missing legacy Apple revocation credentials do not block local deletion; the user can manually revoke Formie in Apple Settings. Apple server-to-server account-deletion events use stored token-custody rows when available and a service-role-only lookup of the Supabase Apple identity for legacy accounts that predate token custody. Apple billing continues until canceled through Apple. Individual and 30-day analysis deletion use the same Gemini cleanup path.

After migration `202608310002_apple_authorizations_and_external_deletions.sql` and the worker Edge Function are deployed, run `node scripts/configure-external-deletion-worker.mjs` with `SUPABASE_URL`, `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY`, and the same `EXTERNAL_DELETION_WORKER_SECRET` stored on the Edge Function. The command configures the Vault-backed once-per-minute cron invocation without printing secret values.

## Reviewer flow

Reviewer credentials and contact details are live App Store Connect inputs and must never be committed. The reviewer can use the dedicated adult email/password account or native Sign in with Apple, make the separate AI-processing choice during account creation, open the native paywall, record without microphone access, complete an analysis directly from Set Details, open an external attributed YouTube tutorial, inspect the static Coach Preview, withdraw consent, and delete the account in Settings.

The source declares `ios.supportsTablet: false`, but Apple reviewed Builds 89 and 93 on an iPad Air 11-inch in iPhone compatibility mode. Corrected-build acceptance must therefore cover both a physical iPhone and the iPhone-only app running on an iPad Air compatibility surface. App Store Connect currently also enables Apple silicon Mac and Apple Vision Pro availability; both untested compatibility storefront surfaces must be disabled before submission.

The machine-readable release-surface and screenshot contract is [1.0-release-gates.json](./1.0-release-gates.json). It locks the 6.9-inch canvas to 1290×2796, places the angled-phone benefits composition at the far left, and orders the remaining corrected-build images from recording through progress and the next set. `npm run audit:submission-assets` validates all six required PNGs, dimensions, and Apple's no-alpha/no-transparency rule before upload. The first image has been flattened and visually reverified; images 02–05 and the subscription review image remain intentionally absent until capture from the corrected build. Apple's current [screenshot specifications](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/screenshot-specifications/) list 1290×2796 as an accepted 6.9-inch portrait size and prohibit alpha channels or transparency; the [IAP review reference](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/in-app-purchases-and-subscriptions/in-app-purchase-information) permits a review screenshot matching any screenshot specification the app supports.

## Current Apple policy reconciliation — September 1, 2026

- Apple still requires in-app account deletion for apps that support account creation, and its current Sign in with Apple technote expects server-side token custody and programmatic revocation when credentials are available. Formie retains token custody for new Apple accounts; missing credentials on a legacy account no longer block deletion and are handled with Apple's documented manual-revocation fallback.
- Apple’s current generative-AI guidance says people must know what may leave the device, see what is shared, understand off-device storage/training behavior, grant permission before personal information is used, and retain a clear opt-out. Formie presents AI processing as a distinct account-creation choice rather than bundling it into Terms acceptance, asks legacy users before recording, and retains withdrawal and deletion controls.
- Apple’s current privacy instructions require declarations to include integrated third-party partners and remain accurate. `privacy-labels.json` remains the source of truth; the live App Store privacy answers must still be replaced and published after App Store Connect access is restored.
- Apple currently accepts 1290×2796 as a 6.9-inch portrait screenshot and prohibits alpha/transparency. The asset gate enforces that exact canvas, flattened PNGs, the benefits-first order, and the four chronological corrected-build images.
- Apple says a minimum-age EULA higher than the calculated rating must use Override to Higher Age Rating. The 18+ override and age-suitability URL remain release gates.
- Apple says the first app version, subscription group, and subscription must be included in the same draft submission. Formie 1.0, Formie Pro, and Formie Monthly therefore remain one final submission package.
