# Segmented muscle model attribution

`formie-segmented-muscles.glb` is adapted from the Z-Anatomy human muscular
system by Lluís Vinent and from the filtered `fullbody.glb` conversion in
`sesgigikimo/gym-muscle`.

- Z-Anatomy: https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy
- gym-muscle conversion: https://github.com/sesgigikimo/gym-muscle
- License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Formie modification: Draco geometry was decoded and the meshes were simplified
for native mobile rendering. The muscle names and `extras.muscle` segmentation
metadata are retained. This adapted model is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.
